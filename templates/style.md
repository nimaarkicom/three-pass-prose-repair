# style.md

What the piece has to do, separate from how it sounds. A rule that
changes a word or a sentence belongs in `voice.md`, not here.

## The claim

What this piece argues or shows, in one sentence.

## The reader

Who reads this, what they already know, and what they should think or
do differently after reading it.

## Evidence standard

What has to back up a claim before it goes in: a number, a source, a
firsthand account. What does not count as good enough.

## Done, not just finished

The conditions that make this piece ready, beyond "the words exist."
A checklist, not a feeling.
