# voice.md

How the sentences should sound. Fill this from a few pieces of real
writing by the person, not from a description of their personality.

## Sentence habits

- Typical sentence length, and when a short or long one is used on
  purpose.
- Words and phrases to avoid. Name the actual word, not "avoid
  corporate language."
- Punctuation habits: dashes, semicolons, colons, fragments.

## Register

- Formal or casual, and where it shifts.
- First person or third person.
- Jokes, asides, or a flat delivery.

## Real examples

Paste two or three short passages that sound right. Do not paraphrase
them into a rule; the passage itself is the rule.

## Real counter-examples

Paste a passage that got rewritten because it did not sound like the
person. Say what was wrong with it in one line.
